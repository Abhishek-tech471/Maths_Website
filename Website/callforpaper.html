CALL FOR PAPER – RAMSIA 2026

The RAMSIA 2026 conference will explore recent advances, emerging challenges, and interdisciplinary innovations in the vast domains of Applied Mathematics, including modern theoretical methods, analytical frameworks, and computational techniques impacting science and engineering. The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to discuss new mathematical tools and their applications across various scientific fields.

Tracks / Sub-Tracks for RAMSIA 2026

The RAMSIA 2026 organizing committee invites original research contributions from all areas of Pure and Applied Mathematics, including, but not limited to, the following:

Abstract Algebra
Optimization
Control and Systems Theory
Differential Equations
Nonlinear Analysis
Fractional Calculus
Bio-Mathematics
Functional Analysis
Probability and Statistics
Financial Mathematics
Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
Applied Statistics
Fluid Mechanics
Cosmology, Wormholes & Black Holes
Mathematical Physics
Fuzzy Mathematics
Cryptography & Neural Network
Image Processing
General Relativity & Modified Gravity
Numerical Analysis
Differential Geometry
Operation Research
Other Interdisciplinary Areas of Applied Mathematics

Submission Guidelines

All authors are required to submit unpublished research paper through Microsoft CMT Platform : https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/RAMSIA2026

Important Dates

Abstract Submission: 15 March, 2026
Registration Deadline: 15 March, 2026
Full Paper: 20 April, 2026
Final Acceptance: 25 April, 2026

Note: In case of student registration, only the student author is allowed to present the paper. For faculty registration, both student and faculty authors may present.
